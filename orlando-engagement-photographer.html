Location Guide · Orlando

Best Engagement Session
Locations in Orlando

01
Kraft Azalea Garden

The most beautiful spot in Central Florida for golden-hour portraits. Ancient cypress trees, a gazebo on Lake Maitland, and light that feels like it was made for photographs. Sunrise sessions are breathtaking.

02
Lake Eola Park

Downtown Orlando's iconic park — the fountain, swan boats, and city skyline make for a classic, timeless backdrop. Evenings when the fountain lights are on are especially romantic.

03
Disney Area Boardwalk

Magic Kingdom silhouette at dusk, Grand Floridian waterfront — this area is endlessly romantic and surprisingly photogenic. No park ticket needed for the outdoor boardwalk areas.

04
Harry P. Leu Gardens

25 acres of botanical gardens. The rose garden and lakeside paths offer lush, natural framing that feels intimate and private — even though it's in downtown Orlando.

05
Winter Park Canals

Moss-covered waterways, historic estates, and golden reflections on the water. The canal boat area is one of the most underrated photography spots in Florida.

06
Your Spot

Where did you have your first date? Where do you go on Sunday mornings? The most meaningful backdrop is always the most personal one — and we'll make it beautiful.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know
Before Booking

When is the best time for an engagement session in Orlando?
Golden hour — 60 minutes before sunset — gives the warmest, most cinematic light year-round. Kraft Azalea at sunrise is equally stunning. We'll give you the exact window for your session date based on the location you choose.
What should we wear to our engagement session?
We suggest one outfit that coordinates (not matches exactly) in neutral or muted tones — creams, dusty blues, sage, terracotta. Avoid busy patterns that distract from your faces. We send a detailed style guide after booking.
How long will it take to get our engagement photos?
A sneak peek of 20–30 images arrives in your private Pixieset gallery within 24 hours. The full edited gallery is delivered within 48–72 hours. If you need photos for save-the-dates, let us know your timeline and we'll accommodate you.
Can we include our dog or pets?
Yes — we love it. Dogs, cats, whatever makes this feel like you. Just keep them leashed at any public park, and plan for extra time to get settled shots between the chaotic-but-adorable moments.
Do you offer engagement sessions for The Forever wedding package?
Yes — a complimentary engagement session is included in every The Forever package. It's a great way to meet before the wedding day and build comfort in front of the camera, so you feel natural and at ease when it matters most.
